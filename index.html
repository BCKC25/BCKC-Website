    About me

    Hi, I'm Josh.

    I'm a Navy veteran, and the Navy taught me that the details matter. I bring that same attention to detail to every small batch of popcorn.

    I started Big Cheese Kettle Co. in 2025 right here in Aiken. Our goal is simple: offer a better-for-you snack with a taste you wouldn't expect from popcorn. That's why we make preservative-free and dye-free options that kids and adults can both enjoy.

    I measure, pop, season, and bag every order by hand. When you buy a bag, you're supporting a local veteran-owned family business. Come say hi at the next event.
